    How much does a website cost?

    Starting prices are ₹500 for a Mini Website, ₹700 for a Starter Website, ₹1,500 for a Small Business Website, and ₹3,000 for a Professional Website. Custom projects start at ₹5,000. Your final price depends on the features, pages, design, and functionality you need.

    Is the estimate on this page my final price?

    No. The estimate is a starting point based on your choices. I send an exact quotation by email after reviewing your requirements.

    How long does a website take?

    Mini and Starter websites are usually completed faster than larger projects. Your timeline is confirmed when I send your quotation, based on the scope.

    Will my website work on Android phones?

    Yes. Every website is designed to be responsive, so it adapts to Android phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.

    Do I need to buy a domain or hosting?

    Not necessarily. Sites can be hosted for free on GitHub Pages. A custom domain name is optional and I can help you connect one.

    Can I change the content later?

    Yes. I can make updates to text, images, and prices. Ask me about ongoing support when you request your quotation.
